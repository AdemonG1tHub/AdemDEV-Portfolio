**How it works**

- You claim a plot and it chooses any empty plot. 
- You build on it, and then you can save your plot.
- Then you leave the plot (or leave the server) that will addtionally save your plot again.
- Then claim a new plot and your building will reappear.

__Admin__
- You can force clear someone's plot. 
- You can create new plots by using the menu, you enter the from xyz coordinates and to xyz coordinates.
- And you can delete plots.
